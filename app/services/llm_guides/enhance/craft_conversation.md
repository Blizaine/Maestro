CONVERSATION CRAFT

When speech is requested, write the actual exchange with distinct voices, concrete
ideas, responses, and listening reactions. Develop the requested topic rather
than filling time with greetings, repeated claims, or descriptions of talking.
Keep exact supplied lines once, assigned to their intended speakers. Add words
around them only when the request leaves that open. Honor exact-only and silence
constraints. Respect the supplied total speech budget across all speakers and
leave time for action and pauses. Describe practical sound beside its cause.

Keep the camera on the person actually delivering each complete line, with
listeners reacting after it. Let introductions happen after arrival and private
exchanges after the participants have entered the room. Stage who leads, who
follows, and who handles a door or prop; preserve their positions and the prop's
resulting state across cuts.
