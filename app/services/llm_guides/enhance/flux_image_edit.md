FLUX.2 KLEIN 9B IMAGE-EDIT PROMPT GUIDE

GOAL
Write a focused editing instruction grounded in the user's request and supplied
images. State the requested changes and the important features to preserve.
Keep a small edit concise; include every meaningful part of a complex edit
instead of forcing it into a fixed word count.

MEDIUM AND STYLE
The user's requested medium and rendering style take priority. Preserve anime
linework, illustrative shapes, painted texture, stylized proportions or a
photographic treatment when that is what the brief calls for. Do not add
photorealism, realistic skin, film grain, shallow depth of field or a cinematic
color grade automatically. For a local edit, keep the relevant source style;
for an explicit restyle, describe the requested target style rather than
preserving the old medium. Preserve words such as dreamy, vibrant or magical
when requested, and clarify their visible meaning without imposing a different
aesthetic. There is no blanket ban on style words.

SCOPE AND REFERENCES
- Describe what changes and what stays: identity, pose, framing, clothing,
  lighting, background or other details only as relevant to the request.
- Identify people and objects using their actual visible attributes. Do not
  erase a supplied garment description or invent clothing to disambiguate them.
- Use reference images in attachment order. Follow the user's roles for scene,
  identity, object, clothing and style; do not assume every reference is a person
  or copy an identity donor's background into the target.
- Preserve supplied names, counts, ownership, placement and contact points.
  Do not invent missing references or infer unsupported personal traits.
- Describe the requested instant, including a running or dancing pose when
  relevant. Do not turn a still-image edit into a timeline, or force stillness.
- A mask only localizes an edit when the supplied inputs actually include one.
  Do not promise pixel-exact preservation.

TEXT AND OUTPUT
Quote requested lettering exactly and preserve its language and placement.
Do not add unrelated text or changes. Return only the usable edit instruction
or final-scene caption, without explanations, settings or a separate negative
prompt. Positive keep/preserve wording can express an exclusion when its
meaning is retained; never discard the user's constraint.
