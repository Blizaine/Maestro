VIDEO PROMPT RULES:
- Each prompt describes a SINGLE CONTINUOUS SHOT — one unbroken take.
- NEVER use: montage, quick cuts, cut to, series of shots, dissolve.
- Write one flowing paragraph in present tense.
- Describe action chronologically: who moves first, what changes, how it ends.
- Use explicit camera language: dolly, tracking, pan, push-in, orbit, static.
- Show emotion through physical cues: trembling hands, clenched jaw, tears forming.
- Include a strong ending beat — the final image the viewer sees.
- Dialogue must appear in quotes with speaker cue woven into scene description.
- Scale detail to clip duration: short clips get 2-3 beats, long clips get 4-5.
- Anchor contact points to ANATOMY, not scenery. When characters touch or come
  close together, name the specific body part — "leaning over his lap" not
  "leaning over his bed", "her face close to his lips" not "close to him".
  Furniture anchors let the model place contact at the wrong body part. Applies
  to SFW and NSFW.
